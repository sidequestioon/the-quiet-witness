# Changelog

## v0.2.0 — Prologue (2026-10-13)

The first scene in final art: a quiet evening in the investigator's office, and an envelope.

- Hand-drawn pixel-art office and darkroom
- Lighting and colour grade for each time of day, flickering signs and lamps
- Rainy town outside the office window with parallax
- Opening scene: the prologue in the office
- Envelope close-up: open it, lay out what is inside, read the investigator's thoughts
- Case file: objectives and inventory, opened from the icon at the top of the screen (Tab / J)
- The investigator thinks out loud over hotspots
- Quick fade between rooms
- Ambient sound (rain, clock, footsteps) and paper and interface sounds
- Floppy disk icon while the game saves
- Grey-box placeholders removed from the office

Known: time of day can't be moved on in-game yet; the rest of the case is still in grey-box.

## v0.1.0 — Grey-box prototype (2026-10-09)

First playable prototype. All core systems work with placeholder shapes and test content.

- Flags, clues and unlock conditions
- Time segments with lighting that changes with the time of day
- Walking and interaction in side view
- Office hub, town map and travel with title cards
- Dossiers & archive: phone book search
- Ink dialogue with present evidence and silence
- Case file: evidence cards and conversation log
- Evidence board with red threads and conclusions
- Newspaper that opens every time segment and reacts to the player
- "Call it a day" to move time on
- Courtroom finale with three test verdicts and Tuesday's newspaper
- Save and load with autosave
